KDTube @VERSION@ for Fire OS
========================

  Fire-TV-Stick-4K/   for the Fire TV Stick 4K (and every other Fire TV: Stick, 4K Max,
                      Cube, Fire TV televisions). Fire TV runs 32-bit apps only.
  Fire-HD-10-2023/    for the Fire HD 10 tablet of 2023 (64-bit; also fine for the
                      Fire HD 8 / Max 11 of the last years).

Both devices have 3 GB of RAM or less. The app sizes its video buffer and its caches
for that by itself, gives memory back when the device runs short, and leaves when it
sits unused in the background and the system needs the memory.

Fire TV: allow it (once)
------------------------
1. Settings > My Fire TV > About: select the name of the device and press the
   middle button 7 times. "Developer Options" appears one level up.
   (On older Fire OS versions it is there already.)
2. Settings > My Fire TV > Developer Options: switch on "ADB Debugging".
3. Settings > My Fire TV > About > Network: write down the IP address,
   e.g. 192.168.1.50. The computer has to be in the same network.

Fire TV: install from a computer (the best way)
-----------------------------------------------
The tools are in the folder Android/From-a-PC.
Windows:  run KDTube-tv-installer-windows-x64-....exe, type the IP address, Install.
Ubuntu:   install KDTube-tv-installer-ubuntu-all-....deb, then: kdtube-install 192.168.1.50

The first time the TV shows "Allow USB debugging?": choose "Always allow" and OK.
The installer picks the right build, prepares it for the processor of the device
and starts it.

Fire TV: without a computer
---------------------------
1. Install the free app "Downloader" from the Amazon Appstore.
2. Settings > My Fire TV > Developer Options > Install unknown apps: allow "Downloader".
3. Put the apk of Fire-TV-Stick-4K somewhere it can be downloaded from (a cloud drive
   with a direct link, e.g.), enter the link in Downloader and install.
   Or copy the file to a USB stick and open it with a file manager on the Fire TV.

Fire tablet
-----------
1. Copy the apk of Fire-HD-10-2023 to the tablet (USB cable, cloud drive, e-mail).
2. Open it in the "Files" app. The tablet asks to allow installing from this source
   (Settings > Security & Privacy > Apps from Unknown Sources): allow it, install.
   Or use the tools of Android/From-a-PC with the tablet connected by USB
   ("USB debugging" on: Settings > Device Options, tap "Serial Number" 7 times,
   then Developer Options).

Afterwards
----------
The app is in the list of the apps as "KDTube". On Fire TV: hold the Home button >
Apps, press the Menu button on it to move it to the front.
"ADB Debugging" can be switched off again after the installation.

Sound and picture on these two devices
--------------------------------------
* Fire TV Stick 4K: Dolby Vision files are given to the Dolby Vision decoder of the
  stick. Dolby and DTS sound is handed to a receiver / sound bar that understands
  it; otherwise the stick or the app decodes it.
  (This was tested on emulators, which have no such hardware: not on a real stick.)
* Fire HD 10 (tried on a real one, Fire OS 8): YouTube plays at 1080p, decoded by
  the chip of the tablet. It has no Dolby Vision decoder, such files play as HDR /
  standard video. DTS and Dolby TrueHD files play through the decoder of the app.
  "Virtual surround" is on by itself with headphones or the tablet's speakers.
  "YouTube TV interface" (Settings) works with touch; it needs about twice the
  memory and more processor than the app's own player.

Casting
-------
The cast button of the player lists the screens of the home network, also Fire TV
sticks and Google TV boxes. To play from a phone on a Fire TV with KDTube:
Settings > Remote control.
