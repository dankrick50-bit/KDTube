KDTube @VERSION@ for Ubuntu and other Linux systems (the PC edition, 64-bit Intel / AMD)
==================================================================================

NOTE: these packages were built on a Windows machine and checked for their
structure there. They have not been started on a real Ubuntu system yet.

Ubuntu, Debian, Mint (the .deb)
-------------------------------
    sudo apt install ./KDTube-ubuntu-amd64-v@VERSION@-@STAMP@.deb

"apt" brings the few system libraries it needs. Start it from the menu ("KDTube")
or with the command:  kdtube

Remove:  sudo apt remove kdtube
The settings and the sign-in stay in ~/.config/KDTube until you delete that folder.

Other systems (the .tar.gz)
---------------------------
    tar xzf KDTube-linux-x64-v@VERSION@-@STAMP@.tar.gz
    ./KDTube/kdtube

If it stops with a message about the "SUID sandbox helper", see KDTube/README.txt.

Using it
--------
  Arrow keys, Enter      move and choose, like the remote of a TV
  Esc or Alt+Left        back
  F2                     picture, sound and other settings
  F11                    full screen

First start: YouTube asks who is watching. "Sign in with your phone" shows a code
for yt.be/activate, or choose "Watch as guest".

Play on a TV (casting)
----------------------
The cast button in the bar on top lists the screens of the home network: smart
TVs, Fire TV sticks, Chromecast / Google TV / Android TV boxes, consoles. Click
one: the video that plays here continues there. With a firewall (ufw) that blocks
incoming packets nothing may be found: use the TV code then (on the TV: YouTube >
Settings > Link with TV code), it needs no search.
From a phone to this PC: in YouTube here, Settings (the gear) > Link with TV code.

Video decoding by the graphics card
-----------------------------------
The app asks for it (VA-API). Whether it happens depends on the drivers of the
system: Intel and AMD usually work with the package "va-driver-all" (the .deb
recommends it). With the closed NVIDIA driver video is decoded by the processor,
which is fine up to 1080p and heavy at 4K.

What it doesn't do
------------------
* No downloader (the Android app has one).
* Films and shows that YouTube sells or rents don't play.
* What the player did is written to ~/.config/KDTube/kdtube-player.log.
* "Device media" plays MP4, WebM, most MKV, MP3, AAC, FLAC, Opus. Files with Dolby
  or DTS sound are handed to the player of the system (VLC, mpv).
